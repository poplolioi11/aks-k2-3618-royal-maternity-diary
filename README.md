# 護産廳小日記（1897年，韓國學中央研究院藏書閣 K2-3618） / Hosan-cheong so ilgi [Daily Records of the Royal Maternity Directorate for Consort Eom]: Transcription Dataset

《護産廳小日記》是1897年漢城慶運宮護産廳所編漢文日記，記宮人嚴氏及新生兒的產後照護。七天內分時記載母親的氣候、飲食、嬰兒哺乳、睡眠及排泄，另記承辦醫官、醫女、宮廷傳報、沐浴、儀節及賞給。底本為韓國學中央研究院藏書閣 K2-3618。

Compiled in 1897 at Gyeongungung Palace in Hanseong (present-day Seoul), the Royal Maternity Directorate’s diary documents the care of Consort Eom and her newborn. Entries describe the mother’s condition and meals and the infant’s feeding, sleep and elimination. They also identify physicians and medical women, report bathing, and record court ceremonies and rewards. The Literary Chinese manuscript is Jangseogak K2-3618, Academy of Korean Studies.

References all 10 catalogue images. The diary spans scan 2 left through scan 9 right, 14 faces with 196 rulings. The TXT, JSON and TEI XML preserve column locations, annotations, the cover title and blanks. AI-assisted initial transcription with uncertain readings and source positions. No full human double collation, expert review, or accuracy assessment was conducted.

## 方法與覆核 / Methods and review

由 AI 依固定館方影像逐欄讀字，保留原字、版面與疑讀。正文、原件同欄細書、印界、館方影像來源分層。官署產後紀錄、儀節與賞給保留原欄順序。冊中歷史日期採陰曆原記。人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查及準確率量測尚未進行。實際模型識別與使用量未知。此資料公開供比對與引用，處理程式及影像另存。

An AI assistant transcribed the fixed source images column by column, preserving source forms, layout and uncertain readings. Main columns, small script, ruled page structure, and source references are marked separately. Care reports, ceremonial records, and rewards retain their order. Dates remain in the calendar used in the manuscript. Human proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement are pending. The actual model identifiers and usage are unknown.
